Introduction

Your birthday becomes your deck

5meikaku is a simultaneous-reveal card duel game. You fight with a character (deck) born from a date of birth and a gender.
Share out points with five sliders, then flip cards at the same time and outread your opponent. Play a match against the computer, ten in a row in SURVIVAL10, duels and tournaments with friends nearby, or public decks shared on social media.

Main features

  • Create a character (deck) from a date of birth and a gender
  • Adjust five abilities with sliders
  • Simultaneous-reveal card duels (1_match, SURVIVAL10, local, QR)
  • Duels and tournaments with nearby iPhones (no Wi-Fi router needed)
  • Share decks with public deck codes and QR codes
  • Save, record and export decks (paid features)
  1. Step 1Enter a date of birth and a gender to create a character (SEED).
  2. Step 2Share out abilities with five sliders (BUILD).
  3. Step 3Flip cards at the same time and duel (DUEL).

5meikaku Privacy Policy

MASARU NISHIMURA ("we" or "us") sets out this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") for the handling of users' personal information in the iOS app "5meikaku" (the "App").

Article 1 (Definition)

In this Policy, "personal information" means personal information as defined in Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

Article 2 (Information in the App)

1. To create a character, you set a date and a profile category (male or female) in the App. It does not have to be your own date of birth.

2. This information is never sent to our servers. We do not run any servers of our own for the App, and we do not obtain, view, store or manage this information.

3. The free plan does not provide a way to store this information permanently. However, to keep the App in the state you left it, the App keeps one latest snapshot on your device.

4. On the paid plan, this information is saved on your device.

5. If you have iCloud sync turned on, your saved data is synced through Apple's iCloud. The data goes to your own private database, and we cannot see it.

6. Data you create in the App is stored on your device. This includes characters, decks, your PC NAME, special move names, battle logs, settings and tournament records.

Article 3 (Matches and tournaments with nearby devices)

1. In local DUEL, the App connects directly to nearby supported devices over the local network, including peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, to play matches and hold tournaments. iOS asks for your permission before the App uses the local network.

2. The following information is sent to the devices of your opponents and tournament participants:

  • your PC NAME
  • your basic stats and special move name
  • your actions in each turn
  • information needed to replay battle logs and to record tournament results

3. Your opponents' screens show your PC NAME, not your real name. (Please do not put personal information in your PC NAME.)

4. Communication takes place directly between nearby supported devices, without going through our servers.

5. So that you can play the same people again, the App keeps information about the opponents you played (PC NAME, basic stats and special move name) and your recent results on your device for a limited time. The lists in the App show the last 24 hours.

6. QR DUEL runs only on your device and does not communicate over the network.

Article 4 (Public deck codes and QR codes)

1. You can turn your deck into a text code or a QR code and share it anywhere you like, such as on social media.

2. A code contains your deck's basic stats, your PC NAME and your special move name. For a deck with GHOST turned on, it also contains GHOST play data. A code does not contain the date or gender you set.

3. Anyone who has a code can play against that deck. Please decide for yourself how widely to share it.

4. When you read someone else's code, that deck's information is stored on your device.

5. The camera is used only to read QR codes. Camera images are neither saved nor sent.

6. If you choose "from Photos", the App reads only the image you select.

7. With your permission, you can save card images and tournament result images to your photo library.

8. When you tap PASTE, the App reads the copied text to look for a code.

Article 5 (Purposes of use)

The App uses information for the following purposes:

  • to provide the App's features
  • to provide paid features
  • to respond to inquiries from users
  • to prevent misuse and to operate the App properly
  • to comply with laws and regulations

Article 6 (Advertising and third-party services)

1. Except on the paid plan, the App shows ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • your IP address and the approximate location estimated from it
  • information about the ads shown to you and how you interact with them, and about how you use the App
  • crash and performance information

2. iOS asks whether you allow the App to track you (App Tracking Transparency).

  • If you allow it, Google may use the advertising identifier to show you personalized ads.
  • If you do not, the App does not provide the advertising identifier, and the ads you see will be less personalized.
  • You can change this setting at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

3. For how Google handles information, see:

  • the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. The paid plan shows no ads.

5. Paid features are purchased and paid for through Apple's App Store. We do not obtain or manage your payment information. The App checks your purchase status with Apple on your device.

6. When you export data on the paid plan, the file is encrypted with a key that the App creates on your device from your purchase record. This is so that the file can be imported with the same Apple ID. The file is saved only where you choose.

7. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and statistics. These do not identify you personally.

Article 7 (Information we obtain)

1. When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address and other information needed to respond.

2. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry.

3. We use Google Forms for inquiries, so the information you enter may be processed and stored through Google's services.

4. Except where required by law or where we use a service needed to respond to inquiries, we do not provide or disclose this information to third parties.

5. We do not sell personal information.

Article 8 (Retention and deletion)

1. Data in the App is stored on your device until you delete it or delete the App.

2. You can delete characters synced to iCloud by deleting all files in the App, or from iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

3. Inquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond.

Article 9 (Children)

The App is intended for a wide range of ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent personal information through the contact form, please contact us and we will delete it.

Article 10 (Changes to this Policy)

We may change this Policy when laws are revised, when the App's features change, or as otherwise necessary.

The revised Policy takes effect when it is published in the App or by any other method we consider appropriate.
